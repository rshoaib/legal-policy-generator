## Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

### 1. Duplicate `<title>` and `<meta description>` Tags on Every Page

**Severity: CRITICAL**

Every page renders TWO `<title>` tags and TWO `<meta name="description">` tags. This happens because:
- The Next.js App Router `metadata` export generates server-side meta tags in `<head>`
- A client-side `<SEO>` component (`src/components/SEO.tsx`) also injects `<title>` and `<meta>` tags via React 19 head hoisting

**Evidence (privacy-policy-generator page):**
```
<title>Free Privacy Policy Generator -- GDPR & CCPA Compliant (2026) | Legal Policy Generator</title>
<title>Free Privacy Policy Generator (2026) | Legal Policy Generator</title>
```

The titles also differ between server and client, causing confusion for search engines. Google will pick one unpredictably.

**Affected:** All 141 pages (every page that uses both `export const metadata` and the `<SEO>` component)

**Fix:** Remove the client-side `<SEO>` component entirely. Migrate all its functionality (canonical, OG, JSON-LD, hreflang) into the server-side `metadata` / `generateMetadata` exports. This is the Next.js 15 recommended approach.

---

### 2. Homepage Missing Canonical Tag

**Severity: CRITICAL**

The homepage (`https://legalpolicygen.com`) has no `<link rel="canonical">` in the server-rendered HTML. The client-side `<SEO canonical="/">` won't be seen by crawlers at initial render.

**Fix:** Add to `app/layout.tsx` metadata:
```ts
alternates: {
  canonical: 'https://legalpolicygen.com',
},
```

---

### 3. hreflang Tags Not Rendered Server-Side

**Severity: HIGH**

The `SEO.tsx` component defines hreflang tags for 6 languages (en, es, fr, de, pt, ar), but since it's a `'use client'` component, these are NOT present in the server-rendered HTML. Search engine crawlers will never see them.

**Fix:** If the site genuinely serves content in multiple languages, add hreflang via the server-side `metadata` export using `alternates.languages`. If the site is English-only (with client-side translation as a UI feature, not separate URLs), remove the hreflang implementation entirely to avoid sending incorrect signals.

## Warnings

### 4. 33 out of 36 Static Pages Have Thin Meta Descriptions (Under 70 Characters)

**Severity: HIGH**

Google recommends meta descriptions between 120-160 characters. Nearly every page has a description under 70 characters, which leads to Google auto-generating snippets (often poorly).

**Worst offenders:**
| Page | Description | Length |
|------|-------------|--------|
| `/privacy` | "Our privacy policy" | 18 chars |
| `/terms` | "Our terms of service" | 20 chars |
| `/industries` | "Legal policies by industry" | 26 chars |
| `/about` | "Learn about Legal Policy Generator" | 34 chars |
| `/sla-generator` | "Generate a service level agreement." | 35 chars |
| `/contact` | "Get in touch with Legal Policy Generator" | 40 chars |
| `/history` | "View your previously generated policies" | 39 chars |

**Fix:** Rewrite all meta descriptions to 120-155 characters, including target keywords and a clear value proposition.

### 5. 23 Generator Pages and Most Static Pages Missing Canonical Tags (Server-Side)

**Severity: HIGH**

Only the dynamic `[slug]` pages (industry pages and blog posts) include `alternates.canonical` in their server-side metadata. All 23 generator pages and static pages like `/about`, `/blog`, `/bundle`, etc. rely solely on the client-side `<SEO>` component for canonical, which won't be seen by crawlers.

**Affected pages (sample):**
- `/privacy-policy-generator`
- `/terms-of-service-generator`
- `/cookie-policy-generator`
- `/about`, `/contact`, `/bundle`, `/policy-guide`, `/compliance-checker`
- All 23 generator pages

**Fix:** Add `alternates: { canonical: '<url>' }` to every page's `metadata` export.

### 6. Duplicate Content Risk: `/tos-generator` and `/terms-of-service-generator`

**Severity: MEDIUM**

Both pages have nearly identical titles and serve the same purpose:
- `/terms-of-service-generator`: "Free Terms of Service Generator (2026)"
- `/tos-generator`: "Free Terms of Service Generator (2026)"

This creates keyword cannibalization and splits link equity.

**Fix:** Add a `301 redirect` from `/tos-generator` to `/terms-of-service-generator`, or add a canonical tag on `/tos-generator` pointing to `/terms-of-service-generator`.

## Opportunities

### 9. Sitemap `lastModified` Always Uses Current Date

**Severity: LOW**

`app/sitemap.ts` uses `new Date()` for every entry's `lastModified`. This means every crawl sees "all pages modified just now," which reduces the signal value of lastModified. Google may ignore it entirely.

**Fix:** Use actual last-modified dates, or at minimum, use a fixed build date. For blog posts, use the post's `date` field.

### 10. Missing `SiteNavigationElement` and `BreadcrumbList` Structured Data on Key Pages

**Severity: LOW**

Blog posts have BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, and industry pages have FAQPage schema. However:
- Generator pages lack BreadcrumbList schema
- No SiteNavigationElement schema exists
- No SoftwareApplication schema for the generators

**Fix:** Add BreadcrumbList to all generator and static pages. Consider adding SoftwareApplication schema for the generator tools.

### 11. `robots.txt` Is Minimal

**Severity: LOW**

Current robots.txt:
```
User-Agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://legalpolicygen.com/sitemap.xml
```

This is functional but could be improved by:
- Blocking `/history` and `/profile` (user-specific pages with no indexable content)
- Adding Crawl-delay directive for aggressive bots

**Fix:**
```
User-Agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /history
Disallow: /profile

Sitemap: https://legalpolicygen.com/sitemap.xml
```

---

### 12. No `noindex` on User-Specific Pages

**Severity: LOW**

`/history` and `/profile` are user-specific pages (client-side localStorage data). They are correctly excluded from the sitemap, but they are not marked with `noindex`. If Google discovers them via internal links (they are linked in the footer), they could be indexed as thin/empty content.

**Fix:** Add `robots: { index: false }` to the metadata for `/history` and `/profile`.

---

### 13. Blog Content Loaded Client-Side From Supabase

**Severity: MEDIUM**

`BlogPost.tsx` is a `'use client'` component that fetches content from Supabase inside `useEffect`. However, `app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` uses `generateMetadata` and `generateStaticParams` for SSG, which should pre-render pages at build time. The metadata is server-rendered, but the **article body content** is fetched client-side, meaning crawlers may see empty content.

**Fix:** Fetch blog content server-side in the page component and pass it as a prop to the client component, or convert `BlogPost` to a server component.

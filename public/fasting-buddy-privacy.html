Privacy Policy for Fasting Buddy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Fasting Buddy mobile application (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Use

Fasting Buddy is built as a privacy-first, offline-capable application. We believe your fasting health data belongs to you.

  • Local Storage: All of your fasting data, schedules, and preferences are saved entirely locally on your device. We do not transmit or store this information on external servers or databases.
  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, provide an email address, or enter any personally identifiable information (PII) to use the App.

2. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily for app analytics, crash reporting, or advertising (such as Google AdMob). These third-party providers have their own Privacy Policies addressing how they use such information.

3. Links to Other Sites

This Service may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@orderviachat.com.
